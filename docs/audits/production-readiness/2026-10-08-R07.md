# R07 — loyalty bill regeneration

Date: 2026-10-08, Asia/Kolkata. Starting revision: `0e1e991`, branch `phase-6-infrastructure`.

## Regression evidence

The initial eight integration cases had four real behavior failures after fixing the fixture's invalid hours shape: regeneration changed 15,000 to 20,000 paise while retaining the redeemed points; manual discounts disappeared; a changed phone retained redemption; disabling loyalty still allowed settlement. Further failing checks reproduced conversion-rate drift, inconsistent snapshot settlement, unauthorized manual-discount removal, and invalid integer-money inputs.

Generation, redemption, removal and staff preview now share `computeBillDraft`. Omitted manual discounts preserve the draft; explicit null removes one with the same permission checks. Existing fields are populated in the form. Changing the customer clears verification/redemption; disabling loyalty clears the draft on regeneration and prevents settlement beforehand. Changed conversion settings require recalculation. A blank staff phone keeps the original plan's session-phone fallback.

Settlement verifies redemption units/value, the discounted snapshot and the locked customer's balance before invoice allocation. The ledger and payments remain in the settlement transaction. Printed discount amounts use actual paise, including percentage and reward discounts. Staff, customer and printer readers expose the reward portion as an included amount rather than subtracting it twice.
